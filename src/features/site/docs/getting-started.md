# Getting started

Mneme is a place for everything you study for a course. There are three kinds of page, and they all live in the same folders:

- **Decks**: terms and questions you study with Learn, Flashcards and Test.
- **Notes pages**: readable notes made from your course material, with highlights, annotations and a cheat sheet builder.
- **Pages**: your own writing. An endless sheet of paper for text, maths, plots, tables, code, drawing and pictures.

## No account needed

Open [mnemee.pages.dev](https://mnemee.pages.dev) and start. Everything you make is kept in your browser (IndexedDB) and works offline. When you sign in, what you made on that device moves into your account and syncs to your other devices. See [Accounts, sync and sharing](/docs/sync).

## Your first deck in two minutes

1. Press **Get the LLM prompt** in the sidebar and copy it. Every option is optional.
2. Paste it into any AI chat (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini…) together with your slides, notes or textbook pages.
3. The chat replies with a deck file. Press **Import** in Mneme and paste it, or drop the file.
4. Open the deck and press **Learn**.

[Making a deck](/docs/decks) covers the options, the file format and what happens to a file when you import it.

## Your first page

Press **New page** at the top of the sidebar. Click anywhere on the paper and type. Press **/** or **I** to insert maths, a plot, a table, code and more. [Pages](/docs/pages) covers the rest.

## Find your way around

- **Library** lists everything, sorted by recent, name or progress, as cards or a list.
- **Folders** hold decks, notes and pages. A folder can be split into units like "Week 3" or "Chapter 4".
- Right-click almost anything for its menu: folders, pages, cards, blocks on a page, the paper itself.
- **Settings** has themes (light and dark palettes, accent colours, a custom background), sounds, and Reduce motion.
