# Notes pages

A notes page is a readable version of your course material, written by the AI chat from the same prompt builder (choose **Notes**, or **Both** for notes with a deck linked to them).

## Reading

- **Contents** down the side, with your progress through the sections. Tick sections off as you go.
- **Chapters**: notes can come in parts (one per lecture or chapter). Each is a tab, or read them all at once.
- **Find on the page** with **Ctrl+F**. It opens folded sections to show what it found.
- **Key terms** from the page, and from decks linked to it, show their meaning when you hover them.

## Making them yours

Select text to **highlight** it in one of four colours, add an **annotation** to it, or **bookmark** the spot. They sync with your account and stay private when you share the page.

With a Gemini key, select some text and pick **Explain with Gemini** (right-click, or the spark on the selection bar on phones). Gemini explains it using the passage around it.

## What's on a notes page

Besides text, notes use blocks that Mneme draws in its own style, from plain data the AI writes:

- **Derivations**: steps lined up on the equals sign, with a reason for each step.
- **Plots**: functions and points, with shaded areas and marked points. Formulas are read by a small safe parser, never run as code.
- **Figures**: diagrams drawn as SVG. Mneme rebuilds every figure from an allowlist of shapes and attributes, so nothing in one can run or load anything.
- **Questions** to check yourself as you read. Ones that match cards in a linked deck count toward that deck.

## Cheat sheets

Pick any mix of notes pages and decks, choose what to include (quick references, formulas, key terms, exam tips, worked answers, charts), choose how tight to pack it (two, three or four columns) and the paper size, then print or save as PDF.
