# Accounts, sync and sharing

## Signing in

Sign in with an emailed code or link, GitHub, or Google. There's no password to make or forget. The first time you sign in on a device, everything you made there as a guest moves into your account.

## How sync works

Mneme is **local-first**: the app always reads and writes your browser's own database, so it's instant and works offline. In the background, changes go up to your account and changes from your other devices come down.

- Every change is queued the moment it's saved and sent within a couple of seconds.
- Pages sync one block and one stroke at a time, so two devices working on different parts of the same page don't overwrite each other.
- Deleted things leave a small marker so the deletion reaches your other devices too.
- The sidebar and each page show whether you're synced, syncing, offline, or out of space.

## Storage

Each account has **20 MB** of cloud space, which is a lot of text: a typical page with writing, plots and some drawing is 20–80 KB. Settings shows how much you've used. If you fill it, new changes stay on the device until you free some space, and deleting always works. Pictures have their own **50 MB**, so they never crowd out your writing.

## Recently deleted

Deleting a deck, notes page or page moves it to **Recently deleted** (on the Archive page) for five days, with an Undo straight away. After that it's gone for good.

## Share links

Any deck, notes page or page can be shared as a read-only link from its menu. The link is 24 random characters (144 bits), so it can't be guessed. Your progress, highlights and annotations never go with it. People with the link can read it signed out; signed in, they can save their own copy. You can update the shared copy or turn the link off at any time.

## Your account

The **Account** page has your name and picture, sync status, your linked sign-in methods, and a danger zone. Clearing your decks or deleting your account first emails you a code, and the server refuses to delete an account unless that code was used in the last ten minutes.
