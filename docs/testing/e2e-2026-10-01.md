### Fixed during the pass
- Typed and numeric answers in Learn had no **Check** button, only "Don't know" (Enter was the only way to submit). They now have Check (disabled until something is typed) and Don't know, under the field like every other card type.
- Test setup offered "20 questions" for a 12-card deck. Sizes now only show when the deck is bigger, and a small deck defaults to All.

### Looked like bugs, weren't
- A fill-in-the-blank miss in the stats came from the test script leaving the blank empty, not from grading.
- Import shows only "deck.title is missing" when other things are also wrong. The title check is fatal and runs first; once it's fixed, bad cards are skipped with warnings. Left as is.
